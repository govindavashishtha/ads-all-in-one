## How to read this / limitations

- Rules are stateless: an entity that keeps matching is proposed again every day (each day has its own `dedupe_key`). In production, an executed change starts the 7-day cooldown, so repeats are blocked; here nothing is executed.
- Entity state (status, budgets, learning, `managed`, active ads per group) is **today's**, applied to every replayed day: `entity_history` starts at the first sync, so historical budgets/learning are unknown.
- Cooldowns, manual-change cooldowns and auto-pause counters never trigger (no simulated changes).
- Meta `frequency_7d` exists only for days the `windows` step ran, so `fatigue_flag` is usually false in a replay.
- Escalations (`action=escalate`) go to LLM triage (Phase 4); until then they are listed in the daily digest for a human.
